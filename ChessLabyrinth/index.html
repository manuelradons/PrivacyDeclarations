Game progress and settings on your device

The app stores the following information in local app storage:

  • Your best results for each level. Unlocked levels, stars, trophies and world best scores are calculated from these results.
  • Your selected language and settings for sound, animations and haptic feedback.
  • Completed-level counters and a capped time value used to control the intervals between full-screen ads.

On first launch, the app reads your device's preferred languages to select an appropriate translation. The selected language is stored. The game does not send your device's list of languages to a server operated by me.

I do not operate a server for storing game progress or user profiles. The app does not send me your best results, stars, trophies or game settings. There is no online leaderboard or synchronization between devices operated by the game. The local advertising counters described above are not passed to AdMob as gameplay statistics.

No separate analytics or crash-reporting services are integrated in addition to the Google advertising services described below. The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise device location. Google may nevertheless derive an approximate location from your IP address.

Chess Labyrinth uses Google AdMob and Google's service for managing privacy choices, the User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google is an external recipient of data. The contact for Europe is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Company details are available in Google's legal notice.

The app is configured for non-personalized advertising. Ads should therefore not be selected on the basis of a profile of your previous usage behavior. However, non-personalized advertising does not mean that no personal data is processed: delivering, measuring and securing these ads may also involve identifiers and local storage. Google explains processing for non-personalized ads here.

Depending on the operating system, device settings, privacy choices and ad format, Google's services may process the following data in particular:

  • Your IP address and approximate location information derived from it.
  • Technical information about the device, operating system and app, and device, app or other identifiers where available.
  • Information about ads displayed and interactions, such as ad views, taps or advertising video playback, and technical usage events such as app launches.
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as loading times, crash reports and information about technical errors.
  • Your privacy choices and information needed to manage them.

Google uses this data to provide and measure advertising, for billing, to improve its advertising services and to protect against fraud and abuse. The categories processed in any particular case depend on the conditions described above. Details are provided in Google's information for Android and iOS, and in Google's Privacy Policy.

No additional advertising network SDKs are integrated into the app. However, other service providers and advertising partners used by Google may receive data as part of ad delivery. Information about the providers involved and their purposes is available in the privacy dialog, where provided, and in Google's information about data use on partner websites and apps. If you follow an ad to another website or app, that provider's privacy notices also apply there.

Privacy choices and withdrawal of consent

The app uses Google UMP to determine which privacy prompt is required and to display the corresponding dialog. This check already establishes a connection to Google. It is separate from the subsequent request for advertising. Depending on the region and your choices, authorization by UMP may also allow limited ads to be delivered; it does not automatically mean that you have given consent. Information about Google UMP.

Where consent is required, you can accept or reject the purposes offered in the dialog. Under Settings → Ad privacy options, you can revisit and change your choices if Google provides this option for your region. You can withdraw consent there with effect for the future. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Refusing or withdrawing consent may result in limited ads or no ads being displayed; the game's features remain available.

Consent choices are stored by the Google service. “Reset progress” does not delete advertising consent or data already stored by Google.

Purposes and legal bases

Where personal data is processed and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, the legal bases are:

  • Providing the game and storing settings: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, to the extent necessary to provide the features you request.
  • Advertising-related processing requiring consent: Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.
  • Legally required management and documentation of consent: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.
  • Technical delivery of non-personalized advertising, limiting advertising interruptions and preventing abuse: where consent is not required, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The interests are funding the app and operating it securely with minimal disruption. This applies only where your interests requiring protection do not override those interests.

These legal bases are set out in Article 6 GDPR. A legitimate interest does not replace consent required by law.

Storing or accessing information on your device is also subject to the applicable rules protecting terminal equipment. Where German law applies, non-essential access requires consent under Section 25(1) of the TDDDG. Retention and deletion

Local game data: game progress and settings generally remain stored until you change or delete them. Under Settings → Reset progress, best results and the unlocked levels, stars, trophies and world best scores derived from them, together with the local counters for full-screen ads, are reset. Language, sound, animations, haptics and privacy choices are preserved.

You can remove other local app data using your operating system's data management options or by fully deleting the app. Merely offloading an app on iOS retains its data.

Device backups: depending on your system settings, app data may be included in device backups and restored later. Such backups are allowed on Android. Storage and deletion of these backups depend on your settings and the backup service used, for example Apple's or Google's. Resetting the game does not delete existing backup copies.

Google data: retention periods depend on the type of data, the purpose of processing and legal requirements. Google deletes or anonymizes certain data after defined periods; other data may be retained longer, particularly for security, abuse prevention or legal obligations. Google explains the details and deletion options in its information about data retention. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete data already sent to Google.

Correspondence is deleted once the matter has been fully resolved, unless statutory retention obligations or necessary documentation require otherwise. Contacting me is voluntary; without a means to reply, I may be unable to answer your request.

Your rights

Where the legal requirements are met, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You can withdraw consent with effect for the future. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation; you may object to direct marketing at any time.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the place of your habitual residence, place of work or the alleged infringement. The European Commission provides an overview of your data protection rights.

For matters concerning the app, you can contact me at ch.jog.dev@gmail.com. I have no remote access to the game progress stored by the app on your device. For data that Google processes as an independent controller, the contact and privacy tools in Google's Privacy Policy are also available.

The game does not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect you.

App stores and changes to this policy

When you download or update the app, Apple or Google processes data as an independent controller. Their privacy notices apply to that processing. This policy describes data processing within Chess Labyrinth and when you contact me; it does not replace the app stores' privacy notices.

This policy will be updated if features, services used or legal requirements change. The date at the beginning indicates the date of this version.
